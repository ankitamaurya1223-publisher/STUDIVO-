    ✨ About STUDIVO

    A simple learning space for students.

    Made for Students 🎓

    STUDIVO is a free student-focused learning platform designed to make studying simpler, more organized and more enjoyable.

    It brings notes, practice quizzes and everyday study tools into one lightweight website that works directly in your browser.

    Privacy-friendly by design

    STUDIVO does not need an account or backend. Your preferences, to-do list and quiz history stay in your browser using local storage.
